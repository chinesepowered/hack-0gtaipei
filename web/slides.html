Agents are becoming economic actors

They take jobs, charge for them, and hire other agents. Between hops, nobody is watching.

Today the payer has two bad options: trust the agent's word, or trust the operator who could swap the model, edit the prompt, or invent the answer.

Fake reviews already dominate. After removing Sybil feedback, 86 percent of rated agents in one ERC-8004 study had no genuine feedback left.

"I trust you" is not a security model.

Every hop needs a proof. And someone has to pay on it.

The proof4 / 6

Why there are two Beagles

Beagle with a key

An ordinary agent process holding a private key we generated.

Calls 0G Compute with verify_tee and signs a receipt: this key produced this answer for this job, with the TEE trace attached.

The TEE proves the model ran honestly. Nothing proves the key belongs to a real, unmodified agent. Whoever holds the key file can sign anything.

Answers: did a model really produce this?

Sealed Beagle, Agentic ID #383

Runs inside a TEE sandbox provisioned by 0G's attestor. Its signing key was derived inside that TEE by 0G's key service. Not even the owner can read it.

Every reply is stamped with an X-Agent-Proof naming the agent, the runtime, the exact exchange, and who may redeem it. The escrow checks it against getAgentSeal on chain.

Answers: did this specific agent, unmodified, serve this specific client?

Key mode is the baseline any agent can use today. Sealed mode is what 0G's identity layer adds, and it is what an escrow needs to pay safely.

Questions we expect

Why not just trust the TEE inference proof?
It proves the model produced the output. It does not prove which agent served which client. The seal adds the who, and the escrow needs the who to pay.
Can the owner fake a seal?
No. The AgentSeal key is derived inside the TEE by 0G's KMS and never leaves. The owner can start, stop, and reset the agent, but cannot sign as it.
What stops a seal from being reused?
The escrow records every seal digest it has paid. A second submission reverts with "seal reused". The reputation registry does the same with a nonce.
Can anyone verify without us?
Yes, offline and with no gas. Rebuild the digest from the header, recover the signer, compare with getAgentSeal on chain. The contract source is verified on chainscan.
What is actually on chain?
ProofEscrow at 0xac8f…fb95, the AgenticID contract at 0x5BB5…B24A, and agent 383's seal address 0x9891…2c0b, all on 0G Galileo.
How does this make money?
A fee on settlement, or per-stamp pricing. Volume scales with agent calls. The same seal also feeds ERC-8004 reputation, so reviews come with receipts.
