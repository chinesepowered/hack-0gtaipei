Pinky Promise

The escrow that pays an AI agent only when it can prove it did the work.

Beagle takes jobs. Capybara guards the chest. A cryptographic seal opens it. Built on 0G Compute and 0G Agentic ID.

Agents are becoming economic actors

They take jobs, charge for them, and hire other agents. Between hops, nobody is watching.

Today the payer has two bad options: trust the agent's word, or trust the operator who could swap the model, edit the prompt, or invent the answer.

Fake reviews already dominate. After removing Sybil feedback, 86 percent of rated agents in one ERC-8004 study had no genuine feedback left.

"I trust you" is not a security model.

Every hop needs a proof. And someone has to pay on it.

How Pinky Promise works

1
Client locks a bounty
Task hash, agent identity, and 0G go into the escrow contract. Capybara keeps the key.
0G Galileo, ProofEscrow.sol
2
Beagle does the work
Inference runs on 0G Compute with verify_tee. The provider's TEE signs input, model, and output.
0G Compute Router, 0GM-1.0-35B
3
Beagle stamps a proof
A sealed Agentic ID replies with X-Agent-Proof, signed by a key that exists only inside its TEE.
0G Agentic ID, ERC-7857 #383
4
Capybara pays on proof
The contract recovers the signer, matches getAgentSeal, and opens the chest. Forged or replayed seals are refused.
settleWithSeal, same digest as ERC-8004 reputation
Anyone can verify a seal offline with no gas. The same seal settles payment and earns on-chain reputation.

Live on Galileo today

  • Sealed Beagle is Agentic ID #383, deployed through the 0G attestor. It registered its own signed service from inside the TEE.
  • The chest opened on a real X-Agent-Proof. A replayed seal was refused with "seal reused". A forged receipt was refused with "bad proof".
  • Both 0G products: TEE-verified inference on 0G Compute, and ERC-7857 identity with ERC-8004-compatible proofs.
  • Next: reviews gated by the same seal, multi-hop jobs where Beagle hires a checker, and receipts for customer-facing bots.

Pay-on-proof is the settlement layer the agent economy is missing. 0G is the stack where the proof exists at every layer.
